Delete your Kinhaven account and data

In the app (fastest): open Kinhaven on the parent's phone, tap the three dots at the top right, choose Delete my account, and confirm. This erases the account, every linked child's records, the notification tokens and the family record, and unlinks the children's phones. To remove one child only, open that child's card and choose Remove this phone.

Without the app: email aigamesstudio18@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Kinhaven account". We will confirm by reply and delete the account and all linked children's records within 30 days.

What is deleted: the email address and account ID, each child's name, app list, screen-time records, location and location history, alerts, rules, status, and the push tokens. What may remain for a limited time: copies in Google's backups until they are overwritten, and Google Analytics usage statistics, which expire on their own (up to 14 months).
