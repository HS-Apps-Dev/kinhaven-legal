Privacy Policy for Kinhaven

Effective date: 2 November 2026 Who we are: Ai Games Studio, Lahore, 54000, Pakistan ("we", "us"). We make Kinhaven, an Android app that helps parents and guardians look after how their children use their phones. Contact for privacy questions and requests: aigamesstudio18@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • Kinhaven has two sides. A parent installs it on their own phone and makes an account. A child's phone is linked to that account with a one-time code, after the child has read and accepted a plain-language screen that says what the parent will be able to see.
  • A linked child's phone sends the parent: where the phone is, which apps are on it and for how long they were used, when apps are installed or removed, safe-zone arrivals and departures, battery level, and whether the phone's permissions are on.
  • The child's phone always shows a notification while Kinhaven is running and a screen listing exactly what is shared and every rule the parent set. There is no hidden mode, no disguised icon and no secret installation. Only the parent can remove a linked phone.
  • Only the paired parent account can see a child's data. We do not sell data, we do not show ads, we do not use data for advertising or profiling, and we do not use the advertising ID.
  • A parent can delete their account and every child's data inside the app at any time (section 9).

2. Who this policy covers

  • Parents and guardians (the account holders, adults). Kinhaven is meant to be installed and set up by an adult.
  • Children and teenagers whose phone is linked by their parent or guardian. They do not create an account, give us their name, email or phone number, or accept payments. A parent or guardian decides to link the phone and enters the child's first name or nickname.

Kinhaven is not a service for children to use on their own and we do not knowingly let a child create a parent account. Section 8 explains how we handle children's data.

3.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect contacts, messages, photos, videos, files, calendar entries, browsing history, the contents of other apps, health data, payment card numbers, or the advertising ID. We do not record the screen, the keyboard or the microphone. We do not collect the voice during a call (3.4).

3.4 Live voice call from the parent to the child's phone

A parent can call the child's phone from the app. The child's phone rings and nothing is heard and the microphone is not opened unless the child taps Answer. Audio then travels directly between the two phones, encrypted (WebRTC). It is not recorded and not stored, and it does not pass through our servers. To set up the call the phones exchange short pieces of text (a session description and network addresses) through our database; this is deleted when the call ends. Google's public STUN server (stun.l.google.com) is used to discover the phones' network addresses; it receives those addresses but not the voice. If a direct connection is not possible the call does not connect (we do not currently run a relay server).

4. Who can see the data

  • The paired parent account(s) only. Our database rules allow a parent to read only the children linked to their own account, and a child's phone to write only to its own records.
  • Service providers that process data for us, under our instructions: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, App Check, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Google Play for the app and payments). They are not allowed to use it for their own purposes except as described in their terms for these services.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell data, rent it, or share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect people from serious harm.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Access rules on our database limit every record to the parent it belongs to. The app is set up to prove to our database that requests come from the genuine app (Firebase App Check). We cannot promise perfect security, and no method of transmission or storage is completely safe. If we learn of a breach that affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Usage statistics (parent phone only)

On the parent's phone, Kinhaven uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count a few events: signing up or signing in (and whether by email or Google), linking a child's phone, opening the upgrade screen, starting or completing a subscription (when a purchase goes through, the plan's name, its price and the currency, never your name, email or card details, which Google Play handles), and seeing the "free days are over" screen, plus the standard events the Firebase library records (first open, sessions, app updates). The events contain no name, email, location or app list. Google receives an app-instance ID and basic device information (model, system version, country). We do not use the advertising ID, and we do not use this for advertising. You can switch it off at any time in the app's menu ("Share usage statistics"); switching it off stops collection and resets the statistics ID. A child's phone never sends usage statistics.

8. Children

  • Kinhaven is meant for adults. It is not aimed at children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets). Children do not create accounts.
  • A child's phone is linked only when the parent or guardian creates a one-time code and the child enters it after accepting the consent screen. The parent or guardian who links a child's phone confirms that they are the child's parent or legal guardian and that they agree, for the child, to the collection in section 3.2.
  • Parents can review a child's data in the app, and delete it at any time by removing the child's phone or deleting their account (section 9). Parents can also ask us to do so (section 10). We do not use children's data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than showing it to the parent and applying the parent's rules. We do not share it with anyone else.
  • Please talk to your child before you install Kinhaven. Do not use it to track adults or anyone who has not agreed.

9. Deleting your data

  • Inside the app (parent): menu > "Delete my account" erases the account, every linked child's records (apps, screen time, locations, alerts, rules, status), the push tokens and the family record, and unlinks the phones. "Remove this phone" on a child's card erases that child's records.
  • A child's phone that has been removed stops monitoring at once. A technical anonymous sign-in record (a random ID without name or email) may remain in Firebase Authentication until it is cleaned up.
  • By request: write to aigamesstudio18@gmail.com from the email address of the account, or see https://hs-apps-dev.github.io/kinhaven-legal/delete-account.html. We act on requests within 30 days.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as California's), you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, object to or restrict some uses, withdraw consent, and complain to your data-protection authority. In Kinhaven the parent can see and delete the data in the app. To use any right, or to ask about a child's data, write to aigamesstudio18@gmail.com. We will not treat you worse for using your rights.

11. Subscriptions [Phase 2]

After a free trial of 7 days from the day you create your account, Kinhaven needs a Kinhaven Family subscription. The subscription is bought and billed by Google Play; we do not receive your card or payment details. We learn only whether a subscription is active and when it renews. Prices and renewal terms are shown before you buy, and you can cancel in Google Play at any time. When the free days end without a subscription, the parent's screens are locked, and the linked children's phones stop blocking and stop sharing until a subscription starts.

12. Legal bases (EEA, UK)

To run the service you ask for (account, linking, showing and applying the rules): performance of a contract. For security, preventing abuse and keeping the service working: our legitimate interests. For usage statistics: our legitimate interest in improving the app, with the switch in the menu to object. For data of a child linked by a parent: the parent's consent and the parent's responsibility for the child.
